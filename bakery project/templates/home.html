About La Ultina Bakeshop

Welcome to La Ultina Bakeshop!

La Ultina Bakery was located at the back of ANECO Butuan City. It was founded by Ms. Daisy Gura. She bakes batches of pan de sal and ensaymada in a traditional wood-fired oven. Word of her delectable creations quickly spread, and the bakery soon became a staple for residents seeking comfort in familiar flavors. Over the years, their bakery expanded. They have different branches, including one in Brgy Libertad.

Their branch at the back of ANECO was established in 2021, because they want to expand so that everyone in Butuan can taste how yummy it is in La Ultina.

La Ultina means "The Last" in Spanish, envisioned as a haven where traditional Filipino baking techniques meet heartfelt hospitality.
